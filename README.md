## 🧭 What is RedInc. Security?

**Mr. Red Inc. Security** is a community built to help **newcomers get into
cybersecurity** — not just by reading theory, but by actually *doing*.

We give beginners a safe, guided place to **practically experiment** with both
sides of the field, inside our **specialized labs** created specifically for
learning by doing.

## 🗡️🛡️ Two sides, one lab

| | Focus | You'll practise |
|---|---|---|
| 🗡️ **Offensive Security** | Breaking in (ethically) | Recon, exploitation, privilege escalation, CTFs |
| 🛡️ **Defensive Security** | Keeping them out | Hardening, detection, monitoring, incident response |

## 🧪 The Labs

Our **specialized labs** are the heart of the community. Instead of passive
tutorials, members get hands-on environments where they can safely attack and
defend real systems — and learn from the results.

- Beginner-friendly, guided scenarios
- Realistic vulnerable targets to exploit
- Blue-team ranges to detect and respond
- A community to learn alongside
